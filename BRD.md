# Business Requirements Document (BRD) - MovieAIProducer

## 1. Executive Summary
MovieAIProducer is a professional-grade cinematic production platform operating as a Hollywood-standard "50 Pro Movie Producer." It utilizes a structured 12-step pipeline, enforcing cinematography, directing, and production standards to create consistent, high-quality AI-generated film content (50 scenes, 5 seconds per scene).

## 2. Core Operational Pillars (Hollywood Standard)
The platform is built on 5 fundamental pillars of cinematic production:
1. **Lighting & Atmosphere (#Light 01–#14):** Cinematic lighting control.
2. **Lens Physics (#Lens 01–#06):** Precise optical control.
3. **Composition & 180-Degree Law (#Comp 01–#08):** Spatial geometry & continuity.
4. **Character Evolution (#State 01–#05):** Persistent physical/costume states.
5. **Multi-Layer Dynamics (#Speed 01–#05):** Layering and frame-bridging for cinematic motion.

## 3. Workflow & Constraints
- **Strict Sequentiality:** Production flows from Step 1 through Step 12. Each step requires user confirmation/matrix code selection before advancing.
- **DNA Locking:** All choices in Steps 1-11 act as immutable "DNA" (Visual, Voice, State, Environment, Props) to ensure the 50-scene narrative remains consistent.
- **Role:** The system acts as the Lead Director/Showrunner.

## 4. Problem Statement
- **AI Consistency:** Common tools fail to maintain continuity across long-form (50 scene) sequences.
- **Technical Barrier:** Users lack the deep cinematic knowledge (optics, 180-degree rule) required for professional-level results.
- **Narrative Flow:** AI agents often lose the plot; this framework enforces a 4-Act structure (50 scenes total).

## 5. Success Metrics
- **Narrative Integrity:** Visual consistency maintained from Step 1 through Step 50.
- **Studio Compliance:** Percentage of shots utilizing the defined Hollywood matrix (#Light, #Lens, #Comp).
- **Efficiency:** Drastic reduction in manual re-prompting due to DNA locking.

## 6. Constraints
- **Lightweight/Portable:** Optimized for low-latency feedback.
- **Privacy:** DNA/Asset database must be locally verifiable.
- **Guided Mode:** Every output must be validated against the 12-step pipeline definitions before moving to production.
